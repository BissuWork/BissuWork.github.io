What's in the game

  • Explore a big world: forests, snow, sand, lava and the sea.
  • Chop trees, mine rocks, craft tools, build a house.
  • Cook food, grow crops, find chests.
  • Zombies come out at night – stay near your torches!
  • Make your own character: face, hair, clothes and more.
  • Works on PC, Mac and Android phones.
