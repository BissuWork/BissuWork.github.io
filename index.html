The game

One world. One night at a time.

Start with nothing, build something that lasts, and be home before the zombies are out.

Explore

A big open world with forests, snow, sand, lava and the sea. Wander far, and find chests along the way.

Gather and craft

Chop trees, mine rocks and craft your own tools. Turn what you find into a house of your own.

Survive the night

Zombies come out when the sun goes down. Stay close to your torches and keep the fire going.

Cook and grow

Plant crops, cook your food and keep your survivor fed. Good meals keep you going through long nights.

Make your survivor

Design your own character: face, hair, clothes and more. Or start from the Woodsman, the Explorer or the Casual.

Play anywhere

Windows PC, Mac and Android phones. The game updates itself, and your worlds are kept.

    Help

    Good to know

    I already have the game

    You don't need to download it again. Open the game. When an update is out, the game shows a box. Press Update now and it updates itself and starts again.

    Only if the game says "New version – open download page", come back here and download once more.

    Will I lose my worlds?

    No. Install the new version over the old one and your worlds are kept.

    Windows says "protected your PC"

    That's normal for a new game from an independent developer. Press More info, then Run anyway.

    Something isn't working

    In the game open Settings, then Check & repair game files. It fixes only the broken files. For a bug report, use Copy hardware info in Settings and send it with your message.
